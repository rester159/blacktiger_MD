# v1.5 Arcade attract presentation

Arcade now runs the original ranking/title/demo sequence instead of the Play/DIP menu. Start inserts a coin and interrupts the demo; A spends one credit and starts the original introduction. Up/Down opens DIP switches and B returns to the mode selector. A qualifying Arcade run returns to attract after confirming its initials. The Home menus remain separate.

The converter observes the owner-supplied US ROM in isolated MAME, then builds native display events and tiles. The fixed-bank transition at 0BB8 initializes the bank-7 B000 demonstration input recording; bank-7 8064 advances its input/duration pairs. The witness checks the original E080 transitions at frames 151, 993, and 4186. The port repeats the 4,035-frame presentation between the first and third transitions, including ranking displays, flashing INSERT COIN, scrolling gameplay behind the large title logo, and the demo's death/return. Its credited screen comes from original frame 4900, with the start-button wording adapted to the port's A-button mapping.

This is an observed arcade demonstration replay, not a second gameplay engine or Z80 emulator. It repeats that captured cycle rather than reproducing the original machine's continuing random state across later loops. Mega Drive palette constraints still apply. The observed sequence has at most 44 visible sprite pieces, below the console's 64-sprite frame limit.

The header and ranking cells display the saved score table. Demo playback never invokes gameplay scoring or changes credits/SRAM. Fresh default records retain the original IMO/MKP/M.H/Y.K/Y.F display until the player's table replaces them. Actual saved records, including initials, survive power cycling. Recording a new Arcade score and confirming its initials automatically resumes attract.

Sparse dirty-row updates keep the demo clock synchronized; full-plane transitions retain their timing debt instead of discarding source ticks. Decoder/display workspace temporarily uses the inactive gameplay sprite lookup, explicitly aligned for 68000 word access. Normal gameplay rebuilds that lookup on exit. Lossless atlas packing retains contiguous strips for the hero and ordinary actors and shares smaller blocks in dragon/alternate-color hero rows. The converter verifies reconstruction of all 16,384 sprite cells.

The restored SEGA animation/chant and CAPCOM animation/FM jingle remain only in the local v1.5 cartridge. Their source assets, generated attract data, original-ROM observations, screenshots and cartridges are excluded from Git.

## Validation and remaining performance limitation

Both final builds passed 175 of 176 test scripts. Attract/coin controls, saved rankings, power cycling, new Arcade initials followed by automatic attract return, both opening animations/sounds, the other gameplay regressions, 48 sprite pixel fixtures, all-level performance samples, and strict packaging checks passed. The attract test compares more than 230 native frame states with original scroll/sprite observations through a complete loop and verifies no discarded attract ticks or SRAM changes.

The encounter-pacing test remains below its unchanged threshold. Source-only measurements: level 2 shows 445 presentations per 600 refreshes (44.5 FPS), worst one-second window 26; level 3 shows 471 (47.1 FPS), worst window 23 versus a 25 target. The local build with both openings shows level 2 at 427 (42.7 FPS), worst window 21 versus a 430-presentation/24-window target; level 3 shows 476 (47.6 FPS), worst window 25. Both measurements report zero discarded gameplay ticks, VBlank overruns, and video-cache faults. These are busy-scene samples, not full playthrough or hardware validation.
