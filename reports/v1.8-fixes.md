# v1.8 itch.io feedback fixes

Follow-up: [post-v1.8 hardware-test candidate and Round 8 input evidence](hardware-followup.md). The published binary and results below remain the v1.8 baseline.

Reviewed https://rester159.itch.io/black-tiger-md and both comment pages on 2026-10-19. Prepared locally; not uploaded and no public replies posted.

## Implemented

- Chest trap flames start at the chest's world position instead of searching downward from the current camera or falling back to the player's height. A jump during opening no longer lifts the flames.
- Held weapons draw before the hero body in the sprite table, keeping the idle weapon visible. All five weapon tiers and both directions are checked.
- Round 3 and Round 5 cameras scroll within the actual 384-pixel arenas instead of freezing at an arbitrary activation frame. Round 5's entrance closes only after the hero clears the replacement wall.
- Shop navigation skips owned weapon upgrades. Existing inventory purchase refusal remains enforced. Entering the shop clears the paired bats without clearing unrelated objects.
- FM carriers are attenuated another 6 dB (20 total attenuation steps rather than 12); modulation operators and PSG sound effects are unchanged.
- Included the existing working-tree round-start/restart notice and remaining-lives display, with its runtime test.
- Reduced sprite cache epoch storage by 100 bytes. During verification, the extra static RAM in the candidate exposed SGDK default-font allocation failure before boot; the compact tags restore startup headroom.
- Scoped terrain upload buffers to terrain submission and round reloads, so they no longer occupy stack space during sprite/UI rendering or nest inside one another. A heap walk after gameplay reproduced overwritten heap headers before this change; NTSC and PAL emulator cold boots and 900 attack refreshes now preserve heap integrity, with 1,380 bytes free. Hardware crash resolution is not claimed without physical tests.

## Still open / intentionally excluded

- Modified PAL/60 Hz EverDrive white screen, Model 3 failure, and older NTSC EverDrive/BlastEm reports: software memory faults corrected; physical reproductions and alternate-emulator confirmation remain outstanding. The no-save test failed for the reporter, so SRAM overlap is not established as the cause.
- Round 2 missing rock: all five source falling-boulder rows exist in the converted map; the report lacks a precise location. Skipped per the user’s instruction unless source evidence identifies an omitted rock; placements remain unchanged.
- Round 8 third secret: item exists in the lower of two stacked hidden walls; an input-driven falling attack damages the lower wall, but consistent accessibility needs a dedicated source comparison and natural route reproduction. No placement or hitbox change made.
- Boot logos: the local private kit remains in this build. Source-only checkouts intentionally omit borrowed boot assets; this is not a new regression.
- Dithering/color suggestions: earlier preview experiments remain separate and are not included in this bug-fix build.
- Older reports already covered by v1.7 or prior releases are retained in the existing regression suite. Requests for unrelated game ports are outside this update.
