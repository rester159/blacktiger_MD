# Black Tiger MD v1.9.3

Fix the Round 4 victory sequence jumping to unrelated scenery when the red imp is defeated while hanging from a column after a horizontally wrapped approach. The camera now follows the normal gameplay policy during descent, keeping the player in the arena through landing and the victory pose. The title-screen version reads v1.9.3.
