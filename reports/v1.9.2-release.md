# Black Tiger MD v1.9.2

- Fix Round 5 and Round 7 bosses becoming invulnerable after damage before proximity activation. Clear the stale hit state when the fight activates; applies to normal stages and Boss Rush.
- Fix the Round 3 dragon appearing absent on wrapped horizontal arrivals. Arena boundaries now use the same map lap as the player and dragon, preventing the player from being moved away from the boss.
- Normalize Round 5's doorway entry check across horizontal map laps.
- Update the title/attract-screen version stamp and release filename to v1.9.2.

The earlier Round 3 floating fix remains included. See [investigation and reproduction scope](v191-boss-followup.md).

These fixes were reproduced with controlled encounter fixtures. They do not establish a full natural playthrough or a physical-hardware retest. The original report named a Mega Drive Model 2 VA0 with EverGenesis V3 and Kega Fusion 3.64; those configurations were not available here.
