# Black Tiger MD v1.9.1

Fixes the boss-arena boundary resetting the player's jump state. Holding Jump + Up + Right at the Round 3 right edge could repeatedly launch the hero upward, move the camera away from the arena, and draw the dragon out of view. The controller's horizontal origin now follows the boundary correction while preserving gravity and button history. The same correction covers Round 5's arena.

The on-screen stamp and release filename now use v1.9.1. The stamp reserves three tiles to display patch versions without truncation or overlapping the menu cursor.

The new regression uses actual source boss spawning after an injected approach position and then controller input. It covers both Round 3 edges, its negative vertical map lap, and both Round 5 edges. The exact v1.9 release fails the reproduction. A separate boss failure to spawn before edge jumping was not reproduced. No full natural playthrough or physical-console test is claimed.
