# Black Tiger MD v1.9.7

- Corrected score display to match the arcade, including saved high scores.
- Old-man interactions now wait until the arcade movement checks permit them, including after landing from a jump or fall.
- Undead can be hit while partially emerging, matching the arcade.
- Added community Credits; the opening menu is Home / Arcade / Credits.

Saved scores retain their original internal units and now display with the arcade trailing zero. No save reset is needed. Score-based vitality upgrades retain their previous thresholds.

The reported Round 5 hardware crash has not been reproduced or fixed. Three of that stage's six breakable walls are empty in the original arcade data.
