# Black Tiger MD v1.9.8

Fixes from the v1.9.7 comments by PRETTY MAZE and ZeroVolt.

- **Default high scores:** the initial high score is 20,000 again, not 200,000. The Arcade ranking starts with the original five entries: IMO 20,000, MKP 18,000, M.H 16,000, Y.K 14,000 and Y.F 12,000.
- **Ranking after name entry:** a new score now takes its place among those entries instead of replacing the whole table.
- **Vitality bar:** the bar draws every slot of your maximum vitality. Lost vitality shows as empty slots, as in the arcade, and new slots earned from score appear straight away.
- **Hidden-wall items:** breaking a wall at point-blank range shows its item; you walk into it to collect it, using the arcade's pickup area. The pickup and wall-break sounds play reliably.
- **Dungeon doors:** entering and leaving a dungeon follow the arcade sequence. The game pauses briefly, the screen goes black (showing "WELCOME TO DUNGEON!" on the way in), then "READY" appears before play resumes.
- **Round 2:** removed the extra old man near the start. In the arcade his placement can never appear. The real shop in Round 2 is further on.
- **Armor colour:** the warrior's armor changes colour with its level, as in the arcade: grey, green, lavender, then gold at the top level.
- **Shop:** items you already own are marked with a red X. Prices follow the difficulty setting, as in the arcade: the DIP switch in Arcade mode, Options in Home mode. The default (Normal) uses the arcade's Normal prices.

Saved scores, options and DIP settings carry over. An existing Arcade table gets the original default entries in any empty places; none of your records are removed.

Unchanged: the name-entry screen layout still differs from the arcade. The Round 5 hardware crash reported for v1.9.6 has not been reproduced.
