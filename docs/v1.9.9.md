# Black Tiger MD v1.9.9

Fixes from the v1.9.8 comments by PRETTY MAZE and ZeroVolt. Each report was checked against the arcade game in MAME.

- **After a boss falls:** you keep control while the boss's death animation plays, as in the arcade. You can walk and jump about twice before the round-clear sequence begins. Previously the hero froze the moment the boss's vitality reached zero. You cannot be hurt during this window.
- **Pow and chest traps:** collecting a Pow now puts out the fire pillars from trapped chests, as in the arcade. Flame waves cast by bosses and enemies follow the same arcade rule: Pow puts out their ordinary flames, and the pieces that reverse your controls stay.
- **Camera during jumps:** the view follows the arcade camera. It holds still while you jump and catches up after you land on a higher ledge. As a result, a high jump from the small pillar in Round 6 no longer wakes the golden dragon from below its arena.

Checked and unchanged:

- **Round 1 and 2 boss wake-up:** the port already plays the arcade sequence. When you come within range, the stone faces open their eyes and light up (16 frames). The top block makes its first move about 0.7 seconds later, and the bottom block about 1.4 seconds later. MAME shows the same frames and timing in the arcade.

Saved scores, options and DIP settings carry over.

Unchanged: the name-entry screen layout still differs from the arcade. The Round 5 hardware crash reported for v1.9.6 has not been reproduced.
